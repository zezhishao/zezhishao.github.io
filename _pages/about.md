I am currently an Assistant Professor at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT, CAS), affiliated with the GestaltCog Lab of the Research Center for Intelligent Equipment Systems and the State Key Laboratory of AI Safety. My research interests include time series analysis, spatial-temporal data mining, and AI4Science. Previously, I earned my Ph.D. at the ICT, CAS, under the supervision of **Prof. [Yongjun Xu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=l34KxTYAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN)**, with co-supervision from **Prof. [Fei Wang](https://finleywang.github.io)** and **Prof. [Wei Wei](https://www.eric-weiwei.com/)**.

I have published over 20 papers in top-tier journals (e.g., *The Innovation*, *TKDE*) and conferences (e.g., *VLDB*, *KDD*, *NeurIPS*). My work has been cited more than 2500 times according to Google Scholar. Notably, three of my papers were recognized as the most influential papers by *PaperDigest*, and two of my papers have been featured as Hot Papers in ESI. I have served as a Program Committee (PC) member and reviewer for prestigious conferences and journals, including *TPAMI*, *KDD*, *TKDE*, *NeurIPS*, *ICML*, *ICLR*, *IJCAI*, *MM*, *AAAI*, and others.

Beyond academic research, I am also passionate about open-source software. My GitHub projects have received over 2.7K stars in total, and I am the maintainer of the widely used time series project **[BasicTS](https://github.com/GestaltCogTeam/BasicTS)**.
